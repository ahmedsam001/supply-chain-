  5. Egyptian Geography Substring Matching & Unfabricated Governorate: Enhanced city_to_governorate() with substring matching so compound city strings like 'First Al Sheikh Zayed' normalize to 'Giza' instead of NULL, and removed the fabricated 'or Cairo' fallback in ShopifyExtractor.extract_locations().
  6. Shopify Duplicate Observation & Variant Availability Fix: Discovered Shopify's single-product /products/<handle>.json omits variant 'available' (causing false out_of_stock duplicate observations alongside bulk /products.json). Disabled redundant per-product JSON polling in ShopifyExtractor and updated ChangeDetector to match observations by source_product_id.
  7. CLI --offline-fixture Support & Structured Quality Issue Coverage: Wired --offline-fixture across all extractors in run_extraction.py and expanded DataValidator to emit structured DataQualityIssue records for missing street addresses (LOW) and missing GPS coordinates (INFO) on physical store records.

   - Hard-Blocked (2 sources, Class C DO_NOT_AUTOMATE): btech (robots.txt disallows product paths; checkout API requires session tokens) and amazon_eg (restricted by Amazon Conditions of Use & anti-bot controls). Both raise ComplianceError with 0 network calls.
   - Deferred (13 sources, Class D UNKNOWN): noon, spinneys, samsung_eg, orange_eg, vodafone_eg, we_eg, eand_eg, metro, hyperone, elaraby, talabat, instashop, lulu. All have enabled=false and make 0 network calls.

6. What data fields are reliably available?
   - Product identity & catalog: source_product_id, canonical product_id (UUID5), product_name, brand, category, product_url, image_url, and SKU (via JSON-LD, Shopify variants, or Jumia PDP enrichment).
   - Commercial pricing: current price (EGP), old_price (when discounted), computed discount percentage, and raw_price_text.
   - Availability semantics: availability_status (in_stock, out_of_stock, limited_stock), stock_status (IN_STOCK, OUT_OF_STOCK, LOW_STOCK_SIGNAL, AVAILABLE_IN_STORES, PICKUP_AVAILABLE), availability_scope (GLOBAL, DELIVERY_AREA, PHYSICAL_BRANCH, SELLER_NETWORK), raw_availability_text, and deterministic v2 row_hash.
   - Geographic locations: Tradeline provides 30 full physical branch records with street addresses and verified GPS coordinates across 8 governorates; Jumia provides 27 delivery-zone governorate records; Carrefour provides 19 governorate presence records.

7. What data fields are missing or limited?
   - Exact stock_quantity: NULL across all sources (never exposed publicly without restricted APIs or cart-probing tricks, which are prohibited).
   - Per-branch product inventory (Product X at Branch Y): Not exposed in static HTML or Shopify /products.json; availability is either GLOBAL, DELIVERY_AREA, SELLER_NETWORK, or aggregate PHYSICAL_BRANCH ('Available in Stores').
   - GTIN / EAN barcodes: Only exposed by Dream2000 (inside Shopify variant.sku); NULL on Jumia, 2B, Carrefour, and Tradeline.
   - GPS coordinates on non-Tradeline branches: Missing on Carrefour governorate records, Dream2000 sample branches, and 2B/Raya fixture branches (all transparently flagged as INFO quality issues).
